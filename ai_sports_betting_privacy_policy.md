## 3. Summary of Key Principles

- **Zero Mandatory Personal Information:** We do not require you to provide personally identifiable information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number to access statistical models.
- **Client-Side Data Storage:** All tracked wagers, bankroll settings, favorite leagues, custom model configurations, and simulation records reside locally on your device via standard client-side storage (`localStorage` / sandbox cache).
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** The Application does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise GPS location, biometric identifiers, or personal media files.
- **Offline Functionality:** Core statistical calculators, Kelly criterion utilities, and local portfolio logs function without an active internet connection.

## 4. Information We Process

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
When you log mock wagers, adjust staking parameters, review AI prediction scores, or explore market projections, these data points are saved solely to your device storage. They are never transmitted to external centralized servers.

### B. Technical Diagnostic Information
If the Application utilizes standard platform services (e.g., Google Play Services for distribution or optional crash analytics), non-identifying technical data may be processed automatically:
- Operating system version, screen resolution, and device model
- Crash stack traces and performance diagnostic logs
- Anonymous advertising or analytical identifiers (if enabled by your operating system)

### C. Information We Do NOT Collect
- User accounts or authentication credentials
- Credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial transaction credentials
- Real-time physical location data
